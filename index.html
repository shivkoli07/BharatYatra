THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA
WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC and to secure to all its citizens:

JUSTICE, social, economic and political;
LIBERTY of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship;
EQUALITY of status and of opportunity; and to promote among them all
FRATERNITY assuring the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the Nation;

IN OUR CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY this twenty-sixth day of November, 1949, do HEREBY ADOPT, ENACT AND GIVE TO OURSELVES THIS CONSTITUTION.

✦ CHAPTER I • SACRED GEOGRAPHY ✦
The Sacred Contours of Bharat
From the Himalayas to the Indian Ocean
Bounded by the majestic snow peaks in the north and washed by three vast ocean bodies, Bharat encompasses 28 States and 8 Union Territories connected through sacred river basins, desert dunes, and Western Ghats rainforests.
  • Northern Crown: Ladakh, Kashmir & Himalayan Valleys.
  • Sacred Rivers: Ganga, Yamuna, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Narmada.
  • Peninsular Coastline: 7,500+ km of maritime heritage.
GeographyPAGE 05
✦ CHAPTER II • CHRONICLES OF TIME ✦
5,000 Years of Civilizational Glory
From Indus Valley (Sindhu-Saraswati) to Modern Era
India’s timeless heritage began with advanced urban brick engineering in Dholavira and Harappa, flourishing into the Golden Age of astronomy, calculus, medicine, and philosophy under the Mauryas, Guptas, Cholas, and Marathas.
  • Vedic & Classical Epoch: Upanishads, Ayurveda, Yoga.
  • Imperial Capitals: Pataliputra, Thanjavur, Vijayanagara, Agra.
  • Spiritual Awakening: Birthplace of Buddhism, Jainism, Sikhism.
HistoryPAGE 06
✦ CHAPTER III • LIVING CELEBRATIONS ✦
Festivals: The Pulse of India
Where Faith, Seasons & Folk Art Unite
Every month brings a vibrant celebration. From the sacred fire of Holika Dahan and vibrant dry gulal of Holi, to Varanasi Dev Deepawali and Mysore Dasara royal processions, India's calendar is an eternal festival.
  • Ganesh Utsav: 10-day devotional fanfare & dhol beats.
  • Durga Puja: Kolkata's UNESCO living art pandals.
  • Harvest Joy: Makar Sankranti, Pongal, Bihu & Onam.
FestivalsPAGE 07
✦ CHAPTER IV • MONUMENTAL TREASURES ✦
43 UNESCO World Heritage Marvels
Architectural Milestones of Global Prominence
Home to 43 official UNESCO sites, India showcases the world's most intricate rock-cut temples, stone chariots, stepwells, and ancient universities preserved across millenniums.
  • Rock-Cut Temples: Kailasa at Ellora & Ajanta Frescoes.
  • Engineering Feats: Konark Sun Chariot & Thanjavur Brihadisvara.
  • Recent Additions: Sacred Hoysala Ensembles & Ahom Moidams.
UNESCO SitesPAGE 08
✦ CHAPTER V • INDIGENOUS ROOTS ✦
Geographical Indications (GI Tags)
Certified Treasures Rooted in Indian Soil
Certified under the GI Act 1999, these products legally protect regional mastercraftsmen and traditional farming communities producing items found nowhere else on earth.
  • Iconic Teas & Spices: Darjeeling Muscatel & Kashmir Saffron.
  • Silks: Banarasi Brocades, Kanchipuram & Muga Silk.
  • Folk Goods: Blue Pottery, Bastar Dhokra, Kolhapuri Chappals.
GI ProductsPAGE 09
✦ CHAPTER VI • HANDS OF THE MASTERS ✦
Living Handicrafts & Looms
Generational Knowledge Preserved in Clay, Metal & Silk
Rural craft hubs empower India's cottage economy. Intricate wood carvings, bell-metal casting, terracotta horses, and natural vegetable-dye prints preserve sacred aesthetic geometry.
  • Metal Inlay: Bidriware silver wire carving on zinc alloys.
  • Textile Heritage: Bandhani, Kalamkari & Patola double-ikat.
  • Clay Sculptures: Molela plaques and Bankura Terracotta.
CraftsPAGE 10
✦ CHAPTER VII • UNTAMED WILDERNESS ✦
National Parks & Natural Sanctuaries
Sheltering Rare Fauna from Himalayas to Mangroves
India sustains 75% of the world's wild tigers alongside one-horned rhinos, Asian elephants, snow leopards, and pristine biosphere wetlands.
  • Kaziranga: Greater One-Horned Rhinoceros floodplains.
  • Sundarbans: Mangrove kingdom of the swimming Royal Tiger.
  • Western Ghats: Top 8 Global Biodiversity Hotspots.
