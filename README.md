## 📌 Executive Summary

India possesses one of the world's richest collections of cultural heritage, UNESCO monuments, geographic variety, and regional traditions. However, the modern traveler faces severe friction: fragmented platforms, generic algorithmic recommendations, over-tourism at saturated landmarks, and total opacity regarding indigenous crafts and lesser-known local sites.

**BharatYatra AI** addresses **Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026) Problem Statement SIH26204 (Student Innovation in Travel & Tourism)**. It consolidates travel planning, transit optimization, verified ticketing, regional craft preservation, crowd-distribution analytics, and an interactive AI concierge into a royal-themed, high-performance web interface.
